# Surface brief: home (app/page.tsx)

Mode: Persuade (browse the work, then send a request).
Audience: market regulars wanting a custom/repeat piece, and online gift shoppers who have never seen the work.
Action: open a piece, then "Request this piece" / request form. Proof: her real photos, later Blender 3D spins.
Constraints: no checkout; request is not an order; cat-working.gif stays; logo + Instagram stay; first-person voice.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, confident shop floor in the grammar of a modern apparel store (reference the user gave: pangaia.com). White space and big product frames do the work; refuses the cream-paper, serif-and-terracotta craft-fair look.
OWN-WORLD: Pure white ground, near-black ink, warm grey product frames (#f1efec). One grotesk family (Schibsted Grotesk) at two weights, tight display tracking. Seven category dots pulled from the cat mascot's palette (sage, salmon, tabby, ink-blue, mustard, sky, plum) as the only color. Square-cornered black buttons, hairline rules, no shadows, no textures.
STORY: Visitor sees one maker and her cat, understands everything is made to order, browses by colored category, opens a piece to see all photos (and a 3D spin when one exists), and sends a request with a needed-by date.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim announcement bar (made to order, request not order). Header: nav left, wordmark center, Request right. Hero split: left, huge headline "Heirloom toys for modern times." with one first-person line and a black "Request a build" button plus "See the work" link; right, the cat gif on its own cream frame at full column height.
FORM: Apparel-store grid (pangaia), user-pinned reference; seed a6afe8cc (degraded roll, overridden by user pin).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
