How ordering works

I make each piece after someone asks for it, so there’s no stock to buy from.

  1. Browse

    Look through what I've made for ideas.

  2. Request

    Add pieces to your cart and check out with your details and date. You don't pay anything yet.

  3. Confirm

    I'll email you to go over the details, price and timing. Once you say yes, I start.

  4. Pick up

    I'll let you know when it's done. You can pick it up, or we can talk about delivery or shipping.
